## Boundaries

**Why P and not U.** Only `outbox-recovery` is enabled — the one schedule the dispatcher
depends on, and RT-04's recovery path has no meaning without it. The reconciliation and
maintenance schedules (`payment-reconciliation`, `refund-reconciliation`,
`overlay-session-maintenance`, `event-archive-maintenance`, `audit-archive-maintenance`)
remain disabled and belong to their own rows; `event-archive-maintenance` and
`audit-archive-maintenance` are still `implementationStatus: "planned"` and must not be
switched on at all.

**Enabling a flag in this repository is not deploying it.** Nothing here is evidence that a
Cloud Scheduler job exists, authenticates, or runs. That is deployment evidence and it is
gated with everything else in §32.
